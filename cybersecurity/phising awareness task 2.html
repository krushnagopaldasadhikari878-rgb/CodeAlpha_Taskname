Phishing Awareness Training

Learn how to recognize and avoid phishing attacks.

What is Phishing?

Phishing is a cyber attack where attackers trick users into revealing sensitive information like passwords, OTPs, or bank details.

How to Recognize Phishing Emails

  • Suspicious sender email address
  • Urgent or threatening language
  • Spelling and grammar mistakes
  • Unknown attachments or links

Fake Websites

  • Check URL carefully (e.g., paypa1.com vs paypal.com)
  • Look for HTTPS (🔒 symbol)
  • Avoid entering credentials on unknown sites

Social Engineering Tactics

  • Impersonation (bank, company, friend)
  • Fear tactics ("Your account will be blocked!")
  • Too-good-to-be-true offers

Best Practices

  • Never share OTPs or passwords
  • Verify sender before clicking links
  • Use strong passwords
  • Enable 2-factor authentication
